CUSTOM CARD ARCHITECTURES

Un gran diseño no solo se ve bien; se siente al interactuar. Bienvenido a la colección de botones: una exploración visual que va desde la elegancia funcional de los botones primarios hasta la precisión geométrica de formas personalizadas y componentes de estado como los switches. Diseñados desde cero pensando en la experiencia táctil, la jerarquía visual y el rendimiento técnico en cualquier dispositivo.

Primary CTA

Designed for the primary action within a view or section (Call to Action). They feature dimensions that maintain a clean, strong presence on desktop. On mobile devices, their compact proportion allows two units to be placed side-by-side without cluttering the screen, optimizing space without compromising legibility or hierarchy.

Button Pairs / Dual Actions

These buttons handle binary choices by guiding the user through visual hierarchy (Primary Action vs. Secondary Action). They form a cohesive set that ensures a seamless fit across both desktop layouts and mobile screens. They stand out through a unified graphic language that gives them their own identity as an interactive block.

Switch buttons

Switch buttons execute immediate state changes and toggle interaction modes without requiring prior confirmation. They facilitate fluid interface transitions—from switching preferences like theme (Light / Dark) to expanding mobile navigation. Their instant visual feedback optimizes usability and reduces the user's cognitive load.

Nav-Bar Items

Special components created for desktop environments. They incorporate subtle micro-animations upon cursor interaction (:hover), adding visual dynamism and elegance to the interface without distracting from navigation.
